## 🎯 Mechatronic Linkage Targeting System

**Built a PID-controlled four-bar linkage system to autonomously aim a flashlight at active targets.**

A proximity sensor detected which of five flags was raised, and a motor-driven four-bar linkage oriented a mounted flashlight toward the correct target as quickly and accurately as possible.

### What I worked on
- Designed the four-bar linkage for required angular coverage
- Integrated motor actuation with linkage geometry
- Developed PID control software to minimize targeting time and overshoot
- System testing and tuning for accuracy and speed

### Key design considerations
- Linkage geometry for precise angular positioning
- Minimizing oscillation and overshoot through PID tuning
- Fast response time to maximize targets hit

### Result
- Achieved rapid, repeatable targeting of active flags
- Demonstrated integration of mechanical design with controls
