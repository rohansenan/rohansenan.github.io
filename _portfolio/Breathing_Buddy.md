A music teacher needed a portable device that could inflate a model lung to demonstrate different breathing techniques used while playing instruments. Our team designed a motorized lead-screw driven system that actuated a syringe to precisely control airflow into the lung model.

### What I worked on
- Mechanical design of the motor, lead screw, and syringe actuation system
- Packaging the mechanism into a small, portable enclosure
- Integration of tubing interface between actuator and lung model
- Prototyping and iterative testing to achieve smooth, repeatable motion

### Key design considerations
- Compact form factor for classroom portability
- Smooth linear motion to realistically mimic diaphragm movement
- Reliability across four programmed breathing modes
- Ease of use for a non-technical user

### Result
- Delivered a functional teaching device capable of demonstrating four distinct breathing modes
- Improved how students visually understood diaphragm motion
