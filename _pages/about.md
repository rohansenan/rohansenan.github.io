# About Me

Hi! I’m **Rohan Senan**, a Mechanical Engineer passionate about designing, building, and validating mechanical systems that solve real-world problems. I love taking ideas from concept to prototype and ensuring they perform reliably in the real world.

---

## My Approach

I focus on the intersection of **design, analysis, and hands-on implementation**. Whether it’s modeling multi-level assemblies in SolidWorks or Siemens NX, running FEA simulations, or iterating prototypes in the lab, I thrive on creating solutions that are **efficient, practical, and robust**.  

I value **collaboration and cross-functional teamwork**, often working closely with manufacturing, design, and testing teams to ensure projects meet performance, feasibility, and timeline requirements. I’m always looking for ways to improve processes and workflows, whether through automation tools, design optimization, or careful experimentation.

---

## Projects & Experience

Some highlights of my work include:

- **University of Michigan Solar Car Team**: Led the suspension subsystem, directed multiple R&D projects, and designed mechanical linkages for solar array rotation, contributing to top-four competition finishes.  
- **Kaleidoscope Innovation (On-site at Stellantis)**: Executed mechanical test and validation on automotive engine and battery systems, developing automation tools that improved reporting efficiency by 50%.  
- **Novelis Inc.**: Optimized manufacturing processes using DOE methodologies and LS-DYNA simulations, reducing setup adjustments by 25%.  
- **Personal Projects**: Designed and prototyped electromechanical systems and a mechatronic flashlight linkage, combining CAD, simulation, Arduino control, and rapid prototyping.
